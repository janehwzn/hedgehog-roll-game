Privacy Policy — Hedgehog Roll 🦔

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Hedgehog Roll is a children's finger-drag maze game. We respect your privacy.

Data we collect

We do not collect any personal data. The game:

  • Does not require an account or sign-in
  • Does not collect names, emails, locations, or device identifiers
  • Does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party trackers
  • Does not access the camera, microphone, contacts, or files

Local storage

Game progress (levels unlocked, stars, pets) is stored only on your device using local app storage. It never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Children

Hedgehog Roll is designed for children and complies with child-privacy principles by simply collecting nothing.
